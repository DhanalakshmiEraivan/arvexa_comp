01 / COGNITION ARENA

APTIQ

Advanced assessment for AI literacy, reasoning under constraints, quantitative judgement and pattern analysis.

40 QUESTIONS50 MINUTES2 ROUNDSSERVER-GRADED
ROUND 01

AI awareness

15 demanding questions on model behaviour, evaluation, causal reasoning, privacy, retrieval and modern ML systems.

ROUND 02

Logic & aptitude

25 non-repetitive problems covering deduction, probability, number theory, rates, sequences and quantitative reasoning.

ASSESSMENT PROTOCOL

Designed to reward reasoning.

  1. One individual attempt with a shared 50-minute timer.
  2. Answers autosave locally and to the server while the assessment is active.
  3. Tab visibility, focus changes and other browser-level signals may be recorded for organizer review.
  4. One mark is awarded per correct response. The organizer portal can review and override marks when required.
  5. Submit before the timer ends. Submission is final.
